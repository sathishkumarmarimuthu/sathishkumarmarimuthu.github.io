About / Research

Computational cardiovascular biomechanics

I study the mechanics of cardiovascular tissues and devices, with a focus on patient-specific aortic modelling, stent-graft deployment, dissection propagation, and vascular growth and remodelling. My work connects nonlinear continuum, contact and fracture mechanics with clinical imaging and medical-device design.

My current appointments span the University of Glasgow, Aarhus University Hospital and Politecnico di Torino, alongside collaboration with TerumoAortic. I also contribute to research on statistical emulation of nonlinear cardiovascular systems.

Research themes

01 / MECHANOBIOLOGY

Aortic mechanics & adaptation

Anisotropic fibre-reinforced tissue models, dissection propagation, and stent-induced growth and remodelling informed by follow-up CT imaging.

02 / COMPUTATIONAL MECHANICS

Patient-specific intervention modelling

Finite-element analysis of stent designs and virtual stent-graft deployment to investigate device–aorta interactions and post-surgical complications.

03 / SURROGATES & INFERENCE

Cardiovascular emulation

Research contributions on deep Gaussian processes and multivariate Bayesian spline-based emulators for nonlinear cardiovascular systems; related training in material parameter identification.

04 / COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH

Clinical & industrial translation

Medical-device collaboration and clinical imaging connect aortic modelling with intervention research; current work is based within the EPSRC Centre for Future PCI Planning.
