About / Research

Patient-specific biomechanics for surgical planning

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Glasgow, working with Sean McGinty in the James Watt School of Engineering and Nicholas Hill in the School of Mathematics & Statistics. My research develops computational models to support cardiovascular and neurosurgical planning, investigate immediate and long-term intervention outcomes, and improve implant design.

I study surgery-induced deformation, vascular adaptation, growth and remodelling, and the mechanical response of biological tissues. My current work combines coronary intervention research within the EPSRC Centre for Future PCI Planning with aortic modelling and knowledge-exchange projects with Terumo Aortic.

During my EPSRC- and Terumo Aortic-funded industrial PhD at the SofTMech Centre (2020–2024), I developed patient-specific computational tools for endovascular aortic repair, stent-graft deployment and device–vessel interaction, collaborating with Francesco Migliavacca and Giulia Luraghi at Politecnico di Milano.

Earlier work at IIT Madras combined fluid–structure interaction during craniotomy with a virtual reality platform for LVAD implantation planning and post-operative assessment, in collaboration with R. Krishnakumar and cardiothoracic surgeon Komarakshi Balakrishnan.

My long-term goal is to integrate patient-specific mechanical modelling with virtual and augmented reality to develop predictive surgical planning and navigation tools for cardiovascular and neurological interventions.

Research themes

01 / CORONARY INTERVENTIONS

PCI & in-stent restenosis

Investigating how the mechanical environment following coronary stent deployment influences vascular adaptation and neo-intimal growth, using growth and remodelling theories, fluid–structure interaction and damage mechanics.

02 / AORTIC ADAPTATION

Aortic growth & remodelling

Developing and validating multiscale models using longitudinal pre- and post-operative CT images from abdominal aortic aneurysm patients to understand adaptation following stent-graft implantation.

03 / COMPUTATIONAL MECHANICS

Patient-specific device mechanics

Nonlinear finite-element modelling, XFEM and anisotropic hyperelastic material subroutines for stent-graft deployment, device–vessel interaction and dissection propagation.

04 / CARDIOVASCULAR EMULATION

Surrogates & parameter inference

Co-authored research on deep Gaussian processes and multivariate Bayesian spline-based cardiovascular emulators, complemented by experience in material parameter estimation.

05 / IMMERSIVE TECHNOLOGIES

Mixed reality for surgical planning

Virtual and augmented reality for surgical training, pre-surgical planning and intra-operative navigation, building on the LVAD planning platform developed at IIT Madras.

06 / KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE

Clinical & industrial translation

Working with clinical collaborators and Terumo Aortic to validate computational frameworks and transfer them towards industrial R&D and, in the longer term, patient-specific treatment planning.

Current & selected projects

CURRENT RESEARCH · PCI

Mechanics of in-stent restenosis

Modelling how coronary stent deployment influences vascular adaptation and neo-intimal growth. The research combines volumetric/kinematic growth models, homogenised constrained mixture theory, FSI and damage mechanics.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Drug-eluting stent modelling

Research interest in incorporating local drug delivery into biomechanical models to investigate its relationship with vascular mechanics and neo-intimal growth.

CURRENT RESEARCH · AORTA

Longitudinal aortic adaptation

Using pre- and post-operative CT imaging to develop and validate multiscale growth and remodelling models following abdominal aortic stent-graft implantation.

KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE

Validation & transfer of aortic models

GKEF-funded collaboration with Terumo Aortic to validate and transfer a vascular-adaptation framework towards industrial R&D applications. Earlier EPSRC IAA work developed the stent-deployment and device–vessel interaction framework.

SELECTED WORK

Aortic dissection & device optimisation

Patient-specific stent-graft deployment and design optimisation, including three-dimensional analysis of critical pressures for dissection propagation published in 2025.

SELECTED WORK · 2026

Cardiovascular system emulators

Co-authored research comparing deep Gaussian processes and Bayesian spline-based methods for nonlinear cardiovascular systems.

SELECTED WORK · IIT MADRAS

Virtual reality for LVAD implantation

Developed a C# / Unity platform used for paediatric LVAD planning and post-operative assessment of inflow cannula orientation using geometries reconstructed from follow-up imaging.

SELECTED WORK · IIT MADRAS

Brain shift during craniotomy

Studied the influence of cerebrospinal-fluid drainage on brain shift and neurosurgical navigation using fluid–structure interaction and image-based modelling.
